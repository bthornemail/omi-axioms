## Abstract

Two rates + 5-slice + Unicode lift.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The two rates**

1. even rate step +2
2. odd rate step +2
3. even rate all even
4. odd rate all odd
5. evens = {8,10}
6. odds = {9,11}

**The 5-slice: invariant under all four moduli**

7. 5 mod ${m} = 5
8. 5 mod ${m} = 5

**The two rates converge at adjacent columns (4 and 5)**

9. even top = 0x40000000 (4-column)
10. odd top = 0x4FFFFFFF (5-column - 1)
11. difference = 0x0FFFFFFF
12. = 2^28 - 1
13. = 268435455
14. odd top + 1 = 5-column
15. odd top below 5-column
16. odd top at or above 4-column

**The lift up to 32 bits**

17. even max within 32 bits
18. odd max within 32 bits

**The 5-slice remains constant through the lift**

19. 5-slice constant after lift

**The exceptional position: 5 is outside the {8..11} span**

20. 5 below the span
21. gcd(5,8)  = 1
22. gcd(5,9)  = 1
23. gcd(5,10) = 5
24. gcd(5,11) = 1
25. 5 divides the even rate's upper modulus
