## Abstract

Unicode lift 0x8 → 0xFFFFFFFF, end-to-end.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The three even bases and their walls (I-131)**

1. 6 -> 6-column
2. 8 -> 4-column
3. 10 -> 5-column
4. 9 -> 5-column - ε

**The three walls: 4, 5, 6**

5. walls ascending
6. wall 4
7. wall 5
8. wall 6

**The alignment rule (I-132)**

9. 16^${n} = 2^${4*n}
10. 2^16 = 16^4
11. 16^10 = 2^40

**The surrogate band 0x8000–0xFFFF**

12. surrogate = 32768
13. high nibble of 0x8000 = 8
14. 0x8 is the surrogate signature

**The four worker slices (I-140)**

15. slice = 2^14 = 16384
16. slice 0
17. slice 3

**The gap delineation (I-133b)**

18. :; size 2
19. <=>? size 4
20. {|}~ size 4
21. 2 + 4 = 6

**The XOR closure with threshold (I-141)**

22. XOR 0..2^${n}-1 = 0
23. XOR 0..1 = 1 (n=1 case)

**The end-to-end lift: 0x8 → 0xFFFFFFFF**

24. 0xFFFFFFFF = 4294967295
25. 0xFFFFFFFF + 1 = 2^32
26. high bit = 2^31
27. upper half = 0x7FFFFFFF

**The three walls partition the upper space**

28. wall5 - wall4 = 2^28
29. wall6 - wall5 = 2^28
30. wall6 - wall4 = 2^29

**The 5-column exception: 0x4FFFFFFF**

31. odd top = wall5 - 1
32. odd top above wall4
33. odd top below wall5

**The full lift: two rates covering the space**

34. 4 segments
35. 3 even
36. 1 odd
37. odd above even low
38. odd reaches up to wall5

**The surrogate band sits inside the 16-bit plane**

39. surrogate high = 0xFFFF
40. surrogate covers to 0xFFFF
41. low half ends at 0x7FFF; surrogate starts at 0x8000

**The lift terminates: no value ≥ 0x80000000 in any list**

42. value 0x${v.toString(16)} < high bit
43. ${name}: top doubles past high bit
