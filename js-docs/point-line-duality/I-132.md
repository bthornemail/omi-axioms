## Abstract

The exponent meeting.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The low powers**

1. 2^4 = 16 (hex base)
2. 2^5 = 32 (C0 band)
3. 2^6 = 64 (64-block)

**The Unicode plane**

4. 2^16 = 65536 (BMP)
5. 2^16 - 1 = 0xFFFF

**The 16^10 = 2^40 identity**

6. 16^10 = 2^40
7. 2^40 = 1.1 trillion
8. 2^40 is past 32 bits

**Every power of 16 is a power of 2 with exponent divisible by 4**

9. 16^${n} = 2^${4*n}
10. exponent ${exp} divisible by 4

**The two powers-of-16 in the set**

11. 2^16 = 16^4
12. 16^10 = 2^40

**The gaps**

13. exponent gaps: 1,1,10,24
14. value ratios: 2, 2, 1024, 16M
15. 2^10 = 1024 (KB)
16. 2^24 = 16M

**The hex-digit boundary alignment**

17. exponents divisible by 4: 4, 16, 40

**The complete chain from 2^4 to 2^40**

18. 37 steps from 2^4 to 2^40
19. chain starts at 16
20. chain ends at 2^40

**Byte alignment**

21. 2^16 bytes = 64 KiB
22. 2^40 bytes = 1024 GiB
23. 2^40 = 1024^4 = 1 TiB (in 1024-base)

**The Unicode plane anchors**

24. ${p.name} = 2^16 points
