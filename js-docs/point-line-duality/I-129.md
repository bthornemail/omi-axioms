## Abstract

Prompt sigils, 64-blocks, 60^n, mod-12.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The two 64-blocks: 0x00-0x3F, 0x40-0x7F**

1. low block = 64
2. high block = 64
3. low ends at 63
4. high ends at 127

**60 ^ n over 0..63 = the comparison operators**

5. 60^0..3 = 60..63
6. <=>? block

**60 ^ n over 64..67 = the brace/tilde block**

7. 60^64..67 = 124..127
8. {|}~ block

**The 16-code block 0x30-0x3F, low nibbles**

9. 0x30-0x3F low nibbles

**The 16 codes mod 12**

10. 0x30-0x3F mod 12 wraps the full ring
11. first 12 = full ring

**The :; group and its nibbles**

12. ':' = 0x3A
13. ';' = 0x3B
14. ':' low nibble A
15. ';' low nibble B
16. ':' mod 12 = 10
17. ';' mod 12 = 11

**The digit-group descents (9876, 5432, 1)**

18. 4 groups of 4 consecutive nibbles

**The crossing at 60 (0x3C)**

19. 0x3C = 60
20. 60^0 = 60 = '<'
21. 60^64 = 124 = '|'
22. 60 mod 12 = 0 (the ring's start)
23. 124 mod 12 = 4

**Atomics.compareExchange at 60 on the two blocks**

24. swap at 60 read
25. swap at 60 wrote 0x3C
26. swap at 124 read
27. swap at 124 wrote '|'
