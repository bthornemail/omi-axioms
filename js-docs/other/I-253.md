## Abstract

The translation fixation in two trilingual inscriptions — which renderings of a name keep its literal, Behistun and Rosetta, by I-229's rule.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The guard, as I-229 states it**

1. I-229: YAHWEH mirrors YHWH
2. I-229: ADONAI raises
3. I-229's correction: an empty base heads nothing

**Behistun: King and Thompson's 37 pairs, English then Persian**

4. 37 pairs
5. each English name once
6. P1: Persia, Parsa
7. P1: Media, Mada
8. P1: Maka is one literal
9. DRYVS
10. P2: Darius raises
11. P4: Arabia, Arabaya
12. P5: fewer than half mirror (${mirrors.length} of 37)

**Rosetta: Champollion's fixations, in Budge's words**

13. P6: KAISAROS, KISRS
14. P6: AUTOKRATOR, A.T.KRTR
15. P7: Alexandros raises letter for letter
16. P7: and mirrors under X as KS, D as T

**The falsifier: the fold is not free**

17. Champollion's fold does not rescue a replaced name
18. Darius mirrors only once Y and V are taken from the Persian
