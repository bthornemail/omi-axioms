## Abstract

YHWH and its renderings — which keep the literal and which replace it.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**Substitutions replace the base (P1)**

1. the base of ADONAI is DN
2. Adonai, 'my Lord', replaces the four letters
3. the base of THE LORD is THLRD
4. THE LORD translates the substitute, not the letters

**Supplying vowels keeps the base (P2)**

5. the base of YAHWEH is YHWH
6. Yahweh is YHWH with vowels supplied

**Yah is the head of the Name (P3)**

7. the base of YAH is YH
8. Yah: the first two letters

**Jah and Jehovah: the fold decides (P4)**

9. the base of JEHOVAH is JHVH
10. the base of JAH is JH
11. letter for letter, J and V are not Y and W
12. nor is Jah Yah, letter for letter
13. the fold takes JHVH to YHWH
14. under the fold, Jehovah is the four letters
15. and Jah is Yah
16. the fold is narrow: it rescues no substitution

**The two codices: same letters, one mark apart (P5)**

17. both are יהוה
18. the consonant base is one literal: MIRROR
19. the vowel layers differ by exactly one mark: holam
20. MAM carries one mark more than Leningrad
21. MAM's marks read E, O, A
22. the vowels of JEHOVAH, in order
23. Leningrad's pointing gives no O

**Yah in Hebrew is the head of the Name (P6)**

24. the letters of יָהּ are יה
25. יה is the head of יהוה: MIRROR (prefix)

**The empty base heads nothing but itself**

26. IOU has no consonants: its base is empty
27. the digits fall away: the base of 1234 is empty
28. an empty base does not mirror YHWH
29. nor does a base of digits only
30. the empty base mirrors only the empty base
