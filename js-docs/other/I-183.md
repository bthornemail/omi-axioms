## Abstract

19 closes the first sexy set after the exceptional one, on 5², before the 210 ladder.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The exceptional set, and why it is the only one**

1. the exceptional set: five primes, steps of 6, from 5

**The first other set: (7, 13, 19), closed by 5²**

2. after the exceptional set, the next sexy set of three or more starts at 7
3. (7, 13, 19)
4. it stops at 19 because 19 + 6 = 25 = 5 x 5

**19 is in the sextuplet before the 210 ladder**

5. the prime sextuplets start at 7, then 97, …
6. the first is {7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23}, with 19 in it
7. the first is before the ladder
