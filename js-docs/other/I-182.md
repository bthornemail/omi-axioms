## Abstract

The atlas, 7! = 5040, and where its rules break: the values are right, two indices are not.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**7! = 5040: the Fano plane, relabelled every way**

1. 7 lines
2. 7! = 5040 relabellings
3. 168 of them give the same plane back
4. the rest give 30 distinct Fano planes
5. 5040 = 168 x 30: every plane with its symmetries
6. 720 = 7!/7 = 6!, the orderings that keep one point (GF(2)^3 has 7 non-zero vectors, not 6)

**The atlas slot: f*720 + t*180 + p covers 5040 once**

7. 7 x 4 x 180 tuples, 5040 slots, each once
8. the last slot is 5039
9. 4320 starts the fano = 6 block
10. the first form, t*240 with four t's, overlaps itself

**The row 5040/7m: 120 sits at 42, not 28**

11. 5040/28 = 180 = 12*5*3 (the note has 120 = 12*5*2)
12. 120 is the value at 42: the note's row 12, 6, 4, 2 is 12/m for m = 1, 2, 3, 6
13. 35 (m = 5) is the gap: 144 = 12 x 12, no factor 5 left
14. and the row runs on past it, to 60 at 84

**37 and every prime above 7**

15. 5040 mod 37 = 8
16. 8/37 = 216/999, so 5040/37 = 136.216216…

**Q mod 240 is not the phase: the 240 needs a second index**

17. c = ${c}: one fixed point, one orbit of 65535
18. a 240-phase is off by 15 after each full orbit
19. Δ carries the 15 of 240 = 15 x 16
20. and not the 16: 2^16 - 1 is odd
21. Δ keeps Q mod 240 in half the states for c = 0, 0x3c, 0xff and in 1 of 30 for c = 1, 0x1d: Q is not invariant (the bqfObserver says it is)
22. Q = 4 x (15x² + 4xc + c²): Q mod 240 lands only on multiples of 4
23. 45 of the 60 multiples of 4: a 60-place index scaled by 4, not a 240-place one

**Where the algebra stops: 16**

24. dimension 16: |ab| = |a||b| fails
25. (e1 + e10)(e4 - e15) = 0: the sedenions have zero divisors
26. the quaternions do not commute

## Notes

- What it checks: the 7! relabellings of the Fano plane (168 symmetries x 30 planes); the slot f*720 + t*180 + p covering 5040 once; the row 5040/7m (a multiple of 60 iff m divides 12); the primes above 7; the orbits of Δ (one fixed point, one orbit of 65535) and what a phase on them can carry; how often Δ keeps Q mod 240; where Cayley-Dickson stops multiplying lengths, and a sedenion zero divisor.
- Two claims of the notes fail, each by index, not value: 120 is 5040/42, written at 28 (5040/28 = 180); and 240 = 15 x 16, of which Δ's orbit of 2^16 - 1 carries the 15 only, so Q mod 240 cannot be its phase (Q is also not invariant under Δ, as the bqfObserver says).
- What it does not check: the 240-LED clock, the 60 Klein points and the light messages.
