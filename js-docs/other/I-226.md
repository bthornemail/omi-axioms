## Abstract

RGBA — the three channels and the alpha, the full view of the pixel.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**Two of the three make a difference, not a witness**

1. ${a} ^ ${b} lands in the control column: a difference, unreadable

**All three close to EXHTE: the witness, the full colour**

2. the three fold to EXHTE (I-207)
3. EXHTE is a tag: readable, a witness, not a control code

**RGB is three channels; the pixel's full state is four, RGBA**

4. three channels: every colour of the cube (I-224)
5. RGB plus alpha: four channels, the tetragrammaton's four
6. RGBA is 32 bits, four bytes

**Alpha is not a colour: it is the visibility, the space between**

7. alpha 0: fully transparent, the void, nothing shown
8. alpha 255: fully opaque, all ones, fully present
9. each channel is 8 bits: 256 levels — the 222 as 2^8, the depth including alpha
10. to be seen a pixel needs alpha > 0: the Yes before the null

**The numbers as the author reads them**

11. 223 = 0xDF = ~0x20: the space inverted — the between (the predicate is the space, I-217)
12. 222 = 0xDE; 223 = 0xDF, one bit apart (0x01)
13. 223 is 222 plus the low bit: the gap opens by one
