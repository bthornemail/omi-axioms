## Abstract

The dominoes' empty places, the stella octangula they make and the Fano plane of its moves.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The places read: each place of a half, the values holding a pip there**

1. each half of value ${v} holds ${v} pips
2. vertical: row0col1, row2col1, row3col1, row5col1 never hold a pip
3. horizontal: row1 col0, col2, col3, col5 never hold a pip
4. a half uses seven of its nine places, a tile fourteen of eighteen
5. a quarter turn carries each vertical place to the horizontal place of the same values, high to left

**The moves of the 8 empty dots: three bits, the Fano plane**

6. D is the quarter turn on the empty dots
7. the 8 moves distinct
8. each its own inverse
9. all commuting
10. from any dot, each move to a different dot
11. the 7 moves, the Fano plane: 7 lines, the triples composing to none
12. the kept line, the moves keeping each orientation: T W TW
13. the 4 off it reach the 4 horizontal dots: the tetrahedron the other orientation's empty dots

**The cube and its dark and light tetrahedra: the stella octangula**

14. joined by the turning moves, the 8 dots are a cube's corners
15. its two colours: the vertical dots and the horizontal, dark and light
16. each colour's 4 joined by face diagonals: two tetrahedra
17. every face of the cube holds 2 of each

**4!: the tetrahedron's flags, the Fano plane's 24, the Hasse diagram's chains**

18. vertex to face: 12
19. edge to face: 12
20. flags, a vertex in an edge in a face: 24, 4!
21. the Fano plane's symmetries: 168
22. keeping a line: 168 / 7 = 24
23. and on the 4 points off it, every one of the 4! orders: the tetrahedron's
24. opposite edges meet the kept line at the same point
25. the 16 rules, 32 one-bit steps
26. the Hasse diagram's chains from FALSE to TRUE: 24, the flags
27. 2! x 2! = 4 through each weight-2 rule: a half's two dots, then the other's
28. of the 4! orders of a tile's 4 dots, 2! x 2! take the high half's first

**Against a reading: the seven places are not a Fano plane by straight lines**

29. found: the seven places hold five straight lines
30. six pairs on no line
31. and no line can be added: not a Fano plane as drawn

**The falsifier: W replaced by a flip of one half alone**

32. one half flipped alone: the moves no longer commute
33. and make no three bits, no Fano plane
