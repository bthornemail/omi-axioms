## Abstract

The proof is in the contradiction, not the answer.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The answer is time-dependent**

1. at t=0, the answer is yes
2. at t=1, the answer is no

**The answer is the derivative (I-165, I-167)**

3. derivative at 0 is 1
4. derivative at π/2 is 0

**So the answer cannot be the proof**

5. the answer is time-dependent

**The contradiction is time-independent**

6. "${b.condition}" closes

**The contradiction is the same at every phase**

7. contradiction at phase ${phase}

**The proof is in the contradiction**

8. the proof is in the contradiction
9. the proof is not in the answer

**The measure of proof is the size of the contradiction**

10. both branches close → proof = 1
11. one branch closes → proof = 0.5

**The Catch-22 of "does God exist?" has a total contradiction**

12. the Catch-22's contradiction is total

**The Catch-22 is the same at every phase**

13. Catch-22 measure at phase ${p}

**The answer changes; the contradiction doesn't**

14. some answers are yes
15. some answers are no
16. contradiction at phase ${s.phase}

**The proof is ascertainable by the contradiction**

17. sees the contradiction → proof = 1
18. doesn't see → proof = 0

**The ministry: show the contradiction**

19. the ministry shows the contradiction
20. not the answer
21. because the answer is time-dependent

**The contradiction is the closure**

22. branch 1 closes
23. branch 2 closes
24. the closure is time-independent

**The whole picture**

25. the answer is time-dependent
26. the contradiction is not
27. the proof is in the contradiction
28. the measure is the contradiction
