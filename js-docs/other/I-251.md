## Abstract

A transcription of any text to Braille's 6-dot and 8-dot constants, Aegean vowels carrying the diacritics.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The 6-dot constants: Braille ASCII**

1. 64 cells, 64 characters
2. every ASCII character 0x20 to 0x5F once
3. A dot 1, B dots 12, C dots 14, K dots 13: the standard letters
4. letters, digits and punctuation six-dot cells: dots 7 and 8 clear

**The 8-dot constants: dot 7 a capital**

5. each capital its letter's cell with dot 7
6. 26 capitals, 26 cells

**The Aegean vowels carry the diacritics**

7. Linear B's five vowels, A E I O U
8. no block of marks past 256: one cell holds a mark's place
9. 248 nonspacing marks in the five blocks, each carried by its block's vowel and one cell
10. é: e, then the vowel A and the cell 1, the acute U+0301

**Every text reads back**

11. and composed again, the text
12. every carried mark after a letter, read back
13. every one of the 1,112,064 scalar values reads back to itself

**The constants**

14. the whole: 256 Braille cells, 5 Aegean vowels, 17 Aegean plane signs, 278 constants

**In WSN: the 6-dot the literal, the 8-dot letter, the Aegean character**

15. `assert.equal(ts[i++]![3], close);`
16. the literal's first cell, the blank, is Braille ASCII's space
17. the space's four 8-dot forms, dots 7 and 8 off or on
18. each 6-dot cell has four 8-dot forms: 64 x 4 = 256, the letters
19. every scalar value's transcription a sentence
20. not sentences: a vowel alone, a plane sign with one letter, an 8-dot cell alone that is no capital

**The falsifier: a mark without its vowel**

21. the acute written as a bare cell reads as a letter (cell 1, a): without the vowel the diacritic is lost
22. café without the vowel reads back as cafea
