## Statement

2. six letters: no single centre

**ANGEL: the first five, as the cave's parser reads**

**GLUE in her name**

**The identity as termed pixels**

**Federation: the same tag, the same pixels; and the limit of a colour**

24. but A and I share a colour (blue): three bits cannot name 26 letters
25. so the colour shows the tag, and the tag, the value, is the identity

**G.L.U.E. held: G>L>U>E>, the prompt to pay it forward**

26. the stop (2E) and the prompt (3E) differ by one bit, 0x10
27. the letters held, each stop turned to a prompt: DLE

29. Shift on the stop sets 0x10: the stop becomes the prompt
30. and its neighbour: Shift + , types <, the same bit
31. as 'greater than', only U > E: it is not a ranking
32. as prompts, each letter is passed on to the next: forward

**The shift held is PROPAgation: the AXIOM of PROPAgation**
