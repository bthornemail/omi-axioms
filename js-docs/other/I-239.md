## Abstract

The celestial orbit, 72 — the clock of 1/73, Wilson at its neighbours, the two periods.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**P1**

1. 1008 x 5 = 5040 = 7!
2. 72 x 5 = 360
3. the same five
4. and 5040 = 72 x 70

**P2: Wilson**

5. (${n - 1})! mod ${n} = ${isPrime(n) ? n - 1 : 0}

**P3: the two periods**

6. 1/73 in base 10: period 8
7. 1/73 in base 2: period 9
8. 511 = 7 x 73 (I-194)
9. the repeating block of 1/73
10. is the kernel's B (crystal.py, the author's)
11. and its digits sum to W = 36

**P4: the orbit of 72**

12. 20 = 2 x 10 has order 72 mod 73
13. its powers are all 72 residues: one orbit
14. 8 x 9 = 72, the decimal and the binary periods
15. neither base alone runs all 72
16. 1/59 and 1/61 repeat with periods 58 and 60
