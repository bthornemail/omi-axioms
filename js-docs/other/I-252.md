## Abstract

The Name's 0-sphere — YHWH and YAHWEH its poles, YAH the face; GOD IS|OF WORD as sine and cosine, GOD WAS WORD as the tangent.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**C1. one base**

1. C1: YHWH's base is YHWH
2. C1: YAHWEH's base is YHWH
3. C1: the poles mirror

**C2. the vowels alone**

4. C2: YAHWEH less YHWH is A and E
5. C2: A and E removed in place, YAHWEH is YHWH, the order kept

**C3. the face projects to both**

6. C3: YAH's base is YH
7. the control: ADONAI does not (I-229)

**C4. differently**

8. C4: YAH heads YAHWEH literally
9. C4: YAH does not head YHWH literally
10. C4: it heads YHWH through the base

**C5. the cells**

11. the table: Y is dots 1 3 4 5 6
12. C5: YHWH's cells XOR to dots 1 2 3
13. C5: YAHWEH's XOR is YHWH's XOR A XOR E

**C6. the 0-sphere on the circle**

14. C6: c - r and c + r, the two poles
15. C6: sine 0 at the pole ${p}
16. C6: cosine is the pole's sign, ${p}
17. C6: tangent 0 at the pole ${p}
18. C6: arccosine returns the pole's angle, ${t}
19. C6: arcsine gives 0 at one pole
20. C6: arcsine and arctangent give 0 at the other too: they cannot tell the poles apart

**C7, C8. the lines**

21. C7: one length
22. C7: they differ only at the copula, places 4 and 5

**K1 to K3, carried**

23. K2: WAS's final D at 11
24. K2: IS's at 10
25. K3: FIRE and YHWH, one cell

**The falsifiers**

26. a pole replaced: ELOHIM's base is not the Name's
27. F1: IS against OF's reversal, the same two
28. F1: OF against IS's reversal, the same two
29. F2: IS against WAS's reversal, the O alone
30. a word replaced: with LOGOS the final D is not fixed
31. the poles' cells differ: the vowels are not nothing
