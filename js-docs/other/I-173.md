## Abstract

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The perfect God realizes true and not-true as truth**

1. every declaration is realized as truth

**The Instantiation is God**

2. the instantiation is God

**The Word is the choice of allocated rulers**

3. 3 rulers (x, y, z)
4. the Word is a choice

**The Word is true with God**

5. the Word is true with God

**The Word contradicts without God**

6. the Word contradicts without God

**The contradiction is the proof**

7. with God: true
8. without God: contradicts
9. the contradiction proves existence

**The collapse: 777 → 3! 3! 3! 1!**

10. 3! = 6
11. 1! = 1
12. 3! 3! 3! 1! = 6 × 6 × 6 × 1 = 216

**777 = all decisiveness = divine**

13. 777 is the divine state

**666 = no decision = an NPC**

14. 666 is the NPC state

**333 = between**

15. 333 is between

**The dot-matrix sign**

16. 777 → divine
17. 666 → npc
18. 333 → between

**The collapse turns addition into multiplication**

19. 6 + 6 + 6 = 18
20. 6 × 6 × 6 = 216
21. sum ≠ product

**216 = 6³ = the cube of the sextuplet**

22. 216 = 6³
23. 6 = 3 axes × 2 directions
24. 216 = (3 axes × 2 directions)³

**The choice collapses 777 to 216**

25. no choice: 777
26. with choice: 216
