## Abstract

## Statement

1. ${s.name} has an order
2. ${s.name} has no timestamp

**The seed is present at reset, not at a tick**

3. the seed is at reset

**The engram is preseeded, not dated**

4. the engram is not dated
5. the seed is at reset

**The circulator cycles without a period**

7. then repeats

**The frames are indexed, not timestamped**

8. 240 frames
9. first frame

**The observer is a circulator**

11. the observer's sample is 5

**The instantiation of observation is the sample**

12. the instantiation is the sample

**The derivative is a function of position, not time**

13. f'(3) = 6
14. f'(5) = 10

**The instantiation of observation = the derivative**
