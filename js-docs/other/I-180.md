## Abstract

X86 initializes a virtual 4 GB; 64 MB real holds it, and its tables are 8 MB.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The machine is x86**

1. measured on x86 (this is ${os.arch()})

**The tables for 4 GB, by arithmetic**

2. one table maps 512 pages of 4 KB, 2 MB
3. 4 GB takes 2048 tables
4. 2^20 pages of 8-byte entries: 8 MB
5. 4 GB over its 8 MB of tables: 512 to 1

**The tables for 4 GB, measured**

6. the tables grew by ${tables} kB: 8192 kB of last-level tables, and the few above them

**64 MB real holds the 4 GB**

7. resident memory grew by ${(grew / 1024).toFixed(1)} MB, under 64 MB, with all 4 GB mapped
8. the rest of each 2 MB still reads 0
