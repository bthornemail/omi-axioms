## Abstract

The algorithmic clock — time as a word of the 65,536 blob, and step recovery proved again.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**P1: the cycle from 0x0001**

1. the whitepaper's 8-cycle

**P2: 0x1D1D turns unevenly; eight states never move**

2. periods under 0x1D1D
3. the eight fixed points

**P3: with B injected every state returns at 16**

4. ${x}: not back after one spin
5. ${x}: back after two spins

**The hands (§7.2), found: their periods hold for 65,280 states**

6. minute hand
7. hour hand

**The cyclic numbers**

8. R for ${q}
9. ${q}: every step recovered

**P4: rotation exactly at full period**

10. ${q}: k R a rotation of R iff T = q - 1
11. 73: the smallest prime of period 8
12. B: the digits of 1/73
13. sum B = 36

**The proof's condition: recovery holds exactly where q does not divide R**

14. ${q}: recovered iff q does not divide R
15. below 2000, q divides R at 3 and 487 only

**P5: the axiom fermat_little at p = 2, a = 1**

16. fermat_little's premises hold at p = 2, a = 1 and its conclusion does not

**The falsifier: without the injection a spin of 8 returns**

17. the bare law returns every state at 8 (I-197), so P3's 16 is the injection's
