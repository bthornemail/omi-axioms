## Abstract

The renamed authorities' witnesses — 0x1E, 0x3C, 0x78, 0x18, and the azimuth's seam.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**P1**

1. D+ XORs to 0
2. D- XORs to 0
3. D+ sums to 0x1E
4. D- sums to 0x1E

**P2**

5. the complement {1,2,4,7,8,B,D,E}
6. the diagonals sum to 0x3C
7. and so does the rest
8. the field sums to 0x78
9. 0x3C << 1 = 0x78
10. 0x3C ^ 0x78 = 0x44

**P3**

11. 0x18 = 24 = 4!
12. the falsifier: with addition, no B gives 0x18

**P4 and the found**

13. eight ones each
14. complements
15. one pair of equal neighbours each
16. and it is at the seam, bits 7 and 8
17. a word alternating throughout has none
18. each byte alone alternates
