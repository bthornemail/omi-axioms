## Abstract

DEADBEEF — the commit that carried the tags, read against the base.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The difference and the agreement**

1. DEADBEEF is a tag: eight letters, all of them hex
2. de88bc2d XOR deadbeef = 00 25 02 c2: the first byte cancels
3. XNOR, the complement: ff da fd 3d

**00 25 02 c2: the letters at their indices**

4. 00 25 02, read in base 10 as indices: A, Z, C
5. and c2: c is at index 2, the 02 just before it (C the 3rd letter, index 2)
6. read in base 16 the 25 is 37, past Z: the letters read the digits in base 10

**The c is the difference of the base**

7. between 0 and 25 the difference is 25, not c
8. between 25 in base 16 (37) and 25 in base 10 it is 12 = c
9. the 2 of 25 times the 6 between the bases

**The 25 is the base: 5 x 5 over the .5**

10. the base 10 is two primes, 2 x 5
11. 0.5 squared is 0.25, exact in floating point (2^-2)
12. a fifth part has no end in base 2: only the 2 of 2 x 5 is exact

**The 25 as I-205's square and I-200's lattice**

13. I-205: the tap code's 5 x 5 square
14. the tap code leaves K out (sent as C)
15. the Latin Polybius square leaves J out (it shares I)
16. two orthogonal Latin squares of order 5 address every cell once: 25, not 26
17. I-200: 0^2 + ... + 24^2 = 70^2, Conway's vector lightlike in II(25,1)
18. the letter left out is the +1: II(25,1) has 26 coordinates, A to Z at 00 to 25
19. and the Leech lattice, w-perp / w, has 24

**Ff da fd 3d: d before a, forward to d**

20. ff as a signed byte is -1: the start before 0
21. d is the nibble just before a: 4 bits before it
22. d (1101) shifted one bit in its nibble is a (1010)
23. fd is ff with the 02 cleared: forward from f to d is the XOR's 02
24. d three times, in da, fd, 3d; the field ends on d
