## Abstract

60^n hex-digit descent.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The orbit, n = 64..83**

1. high nibble crosses 7 -> 6 at n=80

**Low nibbles**

2. hex-digit descent

**The /A-F0-9/ partition**

3. group1 consecutive
4. group2 consecutive
5. group3 consecutive
6. group4 consecutive
7. g1 - g2 = 4
8. g2 - g3 = 4
9. g3 - g4 = 4
10. group1 = letters (C D E F)
11. group4 = digits (0 1 2 3)
12. group2 starts with digits
13. group2 ends with letters

**The comparison operators and braces**

14. 60^0..3 = <=>?
15. 60^64..67 = |}~DEL

**The 0x30-0x3F block**

16. 0x30-0x3F block
17. 0x30-0x3F mod 12
18. full mod-12 ring
19. 4-tail = nibble

**Prompt sigils**

20. ${ch} = 0x${code.toString(16)}

**The 64-blocks**

21. `assert.strictEqual(low[0], 0); assert.strictEqual(low[63], 63);`
22. `assert.strictEqual(high[0], 64); assert.strictEqual(high[63], 127);`
