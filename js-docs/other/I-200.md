## Abstract

Index 777, where all 8 units meet: E7 + 1 = E8, and the lattices around them.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**Index 777**

1. 777 in the 8 units is 511
2. nine bits, all set
3. three full 7s: 0b111, the full plane, three times
4. E7's tag (I-194)
5. one more, and every digit carries at once
6. 0o1000 = 512, E8's tag
7. and E6 is 0o776, one short in the last place

**E8: even and unimodular**

8. every generator has norm 2: even
9. integral
10. determinant 1: unimodular
11. eight generators: the 8 units

**II(25,1) and the Leech lattice: the cannonball**

12. 0^2 + ... + 24^2 = 4900
13. w = (0..24 | 70) is lightlike in II(25,1): w.w = 0
14. the only n up to 100000 with a square sum of squares: 1 and 24
15. II(25,1): 25 + 1 = 26 dimensions; the Leech lattice is w-perp / w, 24
16. 24 = 3 x 8: three times the 8 units

## Notes

- What it checks: 0o777 = 511 = 2^9 - 1, three 0b111, E7 in base 36; 0o777 + 1 = 0o1000 = 512 = E8; E6 = 0o776; the E8 Gram matrix from the proofs' simple roots is integral, 2 on the diagonal, determinant 1; 0^2 + ... + 24^2 = 70^2, and n = 1 and 24 are the only n up to 100000 with a square sum of squares.
- The Leech lattice itself (196560 minimal vectors) and II(25,1) are not built here: only the facts that place them are checked.
