## Abstract

1729 = 7 x 13 x 19, the first sexy set after the exceptional one, and its cubes split it.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**1729 and 91 are the first numbers with two cube sums**

1. Ta(2) = 1729: the first sum of two positive cubes in two ways
2. Cabtaxi(2) = 91: the first with negatives or zero allowed
3. 1729 = 1^3 + 12^3 = 9^3 + 10^3
4. 91 = 6^3 - 5^3 = 3^3 + 4^3

**1729 is the triplet's product**

5. 1729 = 7 x 13 x 19, the sexy set (7, 13, 19) of I-183
6. 91 = 7 x 13
7. 19 x 91 = 1729
8. 1729's digits sum to 19
9. 19 reversed is 91

**The cube sums split it into the triplet**

10. 1^3 + 12^3 = 13 x 133 (133 = 7 x 19)
11. 9^3 + 10^3 = 19 x 91 (91 = 7 x 13)
12. 3^3 + 4^3 = 7 x 13

**A difference of squares measures the gap**

13. ${p} x ${q} = ${mid}^2 - ${half}^2
14. the two gaps of 6 measure as 3 and 3; both together, 12, as 6
15. 19 x 91 = 55^2 - 36^2
16. and 36 = 6^2: the gap, squared
