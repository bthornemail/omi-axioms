## Abstract

The finite spectrum — the tagged literals' max and min order, 7 slices of 8 (HSV is its render).

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The finite spectrum: the max and min order, not the computed angle**

1. 6 of the 8 cube corners have a hue (max != min): the hue hexagon
2. 2 are gray (max == min): black and white, the no-hue axis
3. 3! = 6 strict max..min orders of the three channels: the six sextants

**HSV is the render, computed from that max and min**

4. π enters only as the Leibniz projection: the 360 shows only on the render
5. HSV's hue is 0..360° = 2π: computed, the render — not the finite order beneath it

**7 slices of 8: ROYGBIV and A–G, the spectrum in the octave**

6. Newton's 7 bands, chosen to match the scale
7. 7 notes A to G
8. 7 slices and the 8th: the octave, the return — 7 of 8 possibilities
9. 224 RGB, 226 RGBA, 227 the finite spectrum between and outside them

**The last layer: ordered chaos, the stable fours (NORM-4)**

10. dplus {0,5,10,15} XOR 0 (dplus_xor_zero)
11. dminus {3,6,9,12} XOR 0 (dminus_xor_zero)
12. a stable four and a stable four: NORM-4
13. together the 8 even-parity still words (I-222)
14. dminus = dplus XOR 3: the coset, the chaos — ordered chaos
15. dminus is not closed (3 XOR 6 = 5 is not in it): a coset, not a group
16. the eight left, odd parity — \"not sure what's left\"

**Why: the shared human spectrum, outside any frame**

17. 7 days, 7 colours, 7 notes: the shared human slicing
18. the 8th day is the first again: the octave, the return, the Sabbath's rest and reset
