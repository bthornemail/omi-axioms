## Abstract

0xAA55 — the boot mark, the still points, and NULL NULL squared.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The boot mark**

1. 55 AA at 510 and 511 read as the word 0xAA55
2. the mark is the sector's last pair

**The alternating pair, both ways round**

3. `assert.equal(0xaa, 0b10101010); assert.equal(0x55, 0b01010101);`
4. AA is the 170 of I-221
5. AA XOR 55: every bit differs
6. AA XNOR 55: they agree nowhere, NULL
7. the word against itself turned: NULL NULL

**The still points of the delta law**

8. the linear part leaves 0x5555 still
9. and 0xAAAA
10. eight still words, not two: one nibble repeated four times
11. each is a nibble of even parity, repeated: the nibble parity of the ports (rosetta portFor)
12. 0xAA55, half and half, is not still: period 4 under 0x1D1D
13. and 8 under 0x1D, as every word (I-195)

**NULL NULL squared**

14. AA squared, carry-less
15. 55 squared
16. squaring in GF(2) is linear: the squares' XOR is the XOR's square
17. (AA XOR 55) squared = FF squared = 0x5555: the boot pair's difference, squared, is a still point
18. the word squared: AA's square, then 55's

**The little tetragrammaton, by the four-bit adder**

19. Yod He Vav He: 26 (SRC-14)
20. A A 5 5: 30, not 26
21. they share Yod 10 and He 5; AA55 has no Vav 6
