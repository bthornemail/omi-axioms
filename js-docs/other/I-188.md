## Abstract

19.4.4 → 44J → 5347 → (5, 3, 4, 7) → 19, and the exchange at 19 of 64.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**19.4.4 as (frame, sub-frame, index)**

1. section 19.4.4: frame 4, sub-frame 4, index 19
2. 19 is one of the 64 bytes 0..63

**In base 36, the address is 44J**

3. 4, 4, J: J is the base-36 digit 19

**44J is 5347: the coordinate 5, 3, 4, 7, summing to 19**

4. 44J = 4 x 36^2 + 4 x 36 + 19
5. = 5347
6. its decimal digits are the coordinate 5, 3, 4, 7
7. and they sum to 19: the chain closes on the index
8. 5347 mod 36 = 19, the last base-36 digit
9. but not mod 64 or 256: 35 and 227
10. 5 x 3 x 4 x 7 = 420 = 4 x 0x69, the organ's parity byte (3 x 5 x 7, I-102)

**The exchange at 19 of the 64 bytes: 128 for 63**

11. the exchange at 19 finds 128
12. and leaves 63
13. a second exchange finds 63, not 128, and does nothing
14. 63 stays
15. 128 (2^7, the 7-bit completion) and 63 (2^6 - 1, the 6-bit closure) share no bit

**In the vault's own tags, 44 is I-148**

16. the record tag 44 is I-148 (and 44J would read as I-148j)
