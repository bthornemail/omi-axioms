## Abstract

The Klein configuration from the author's coordinates, and Miquel's cube inside it.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):

1. the orbits
2. exactly one union of orbits: 60 points, each on 15 of the 60 planes, the named three among them
3. 32 with four coordinates, 24 with two, 4 units
4. each plane holds 15: the incidence is symmetric

**P1: the cube's 8 points**

5. P1: [1:±1:±1:±1] among the 60

**P2, P3: the 6 face planes**

6. P2: the face plane among the 60
7. P2: a face, 4 of the cube's 8
8. P2: the face where coordinate j is s
9. P3: 15 of the 60 on it
10. P2: each cube point on 3 faces: (8_3 6_4)

**P4: the stella octangula**

11. P4: 2 of each tetrahedron on each face

**Found: the other 32 of the 64 give a (60_15) too, the same configuration with one coordinate times i;**

12. `assert.equal(K2.length, 60);`
13. found: the other half gives (60_15) as well
14. found: it is the first with the last coordinate times i
15. found: it holds neither P60 nor the cube

**The falsifier: one point swapped between the two halves breaks the 15**

16. `assert.equal(swapped.length, 60);`
17. one point swapped: no longer each on 15
