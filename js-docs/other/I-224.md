## Abstract

JabCode — the palette is the cube, the modes climb 2 → 8 → 256, and one symbol holds 65,536.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The palette is the cube**

1. Yellow is 110
2. seven lines, each a XOR b XOR c = Black
3. Red + Green = Yellow; Cyan + Magenta + Yellow = Black
4. `assert.deepEqual([...Array(8).keys()].filter((i) => parity(i) === 0), even);`
5. even: Black and the subtractive primaries
6. odd: the additive primaries and White
7. Blue XOR Yellow = White: each two-colour pair is a complement

**The modes climb: 2 → 8 → 256**

8. `assert.deepEqual([1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7].map(colours), [4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256]);`
9. 1 bit, then 3, then 8 per module: every mode is reached through the lower ones
10. the 3 bits of Nc take 3 two-colour modules
11. two embedded palettes of at most 64 colours

**The capacity, reproduced from Table 1**

12. ${r.modules} modules at ${r.n} colours: ${r.pn} bits
13. the largest symbol, Side-Version 32: 145 × 145 modules

**65,536**

14. at 8 colours one primary, or a primary and one docked secondary, holds less
15. a primary and two docked secondaries hold it: 80,925 bits
16. `assert.equal(modules256, 20830);`
17. one 145 × 145 primary at 256 colours: 71,417 bits
18. one symbol holds the full 65,536 (the pair mask, one bit a pair)
19. and only by colour: 65,536 bits are more than 21,025 modules carry at 8 colours raw
