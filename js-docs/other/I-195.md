## Abstract

The gnomon, the animation of the logical clock.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The gnomon, as tetra-gnomon-anim prints it**

1. step ${step}: state ${hex(printed[step]!)}
2. step ${step}: SID ${hex(sid)} is the next state
3. the eighth SID is the seed: the animation closes

**Eight frames everywhere: every state has period 8**

4. under 0x1D all 65,536 states return after exactly 8 steps

**The atomic kernel's constant turns unevenly**

5. under 0x1D1D: 8 fixed, 56 of period 2, 4,032 of period 4, 61,440 of period 8

## Notes

- What it checks: polytron's K(p, 0x1D) from 0x4242 gives the eight states tetra-gnomon-anim prints, each SID the next state and the eighth the seed; all 65,536 states have period 8 under 0x1D; under the atomic kernel's 0x1D1D, 8 are fixed, 56 have period 2, 4,032 period 4 and 61,440 period 8.
- The source is omi-files/polytron/tetra-gnomon-anim.c, unchanged; the Agent Commons draws the same eight steps as the dial at its Polytron station (space/src/gnomon.ts).
