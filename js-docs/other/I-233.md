## Abstract

The seven days from the eve — declare, define, separate, and the two generations.

## Statement

The check holds when each of the following holds, in this order (taken from its assertions):


**The eve first (P1)**

1. six closes
2. one close ends each of the six days
3. evening before morning in every close
4. and the seventh day has none

**Declare (P2)**

5. and said, ten times
6. on each of the six days

**Define (P3)**

7. naming only on days 1, 2 and 3

**Separate (P4)**

8. separating only on days 1, 2 and 4

**Good (P5)**

9. no 'good' on day 2
10. twice on day 3
11. 'very good' closes day 6

**The two generations (P6)**

12. seed in itself: day 3's plants
13. and day 3 is not blessed
14. the first blessing is on day 5
15. where the living souls first are
16. be fruitful: days 5 and 6
17. blessing on days 5, 6 and 7
18. seed on day 3, and given again on day 6
19. the seed's day is not the blessing's day
