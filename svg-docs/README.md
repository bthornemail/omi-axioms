# The Fano icons and their Hasse diagrams

Generated by `svg/tools/build.mjs` from the sixteen CC0 icons in `svg/fano` (see `svg/fano/SOURCES.md`). Every number below is read from the icons or computed, and checked when the build runs.

## The icons, read as text

Each file's marked dots are located in page coordinates (each circle's own transform composed with its groups'), and the file becomes a 7-bit mask: bit n - 1 is on when point n is marked. The points are labelled by the `Fano_point_n` files themselves.

### The 16-index

The vault's sixteen Fano constants (`core/src/verified/fano.ts`) are these sixteen masks; `svg/fano/masks.json` is written for that check (`src/testbed/core.test.ts`). Index i reads the icon below. The author, 2026-10-13: "the 16 are the 16-index and the exponent is the index read": 16^4 reads index 4, point 4; 16^10 reads index 10, line 2-7-5; and 4 ^ 10 = 4 + 10 = 14 reads line 2-6-4, the line through point 4.

## The rules as the icons

A two-input rule is its truth table t, bit a·2 + b = f(a, b) (the order the organ uses, `breadboard/esp32`). The constants are the empty and the full plane. The other fourteen, paired with their complements (t and t ^ 15), are the seven points: point p is the pair p / p ^ 15. On every line, two rules from two of its points XOR to a rule of the third.

| Point | Rule | Table | Complement | Table | Hasse pair |
|-------|------|-------|------------|-------|------------|
| empty / full | FALSE | `0000` | TRUE | `1111` | [pair-0.svg](../svg/hasse/pair-0.svg) |
| 1 | NOR | `1000` | OR | `0111` | [pair-1.svg](../svg/hasse/pair-1.svg) |
| 2 | B AND NOT A | `0100` | B IMPLIES A | `1011` | [pair-2.svg](../svg/hasse/pair-2.svg) |
| 3 | NOT A | `1100` | A | `0011` | [pair-3.svg](../svg/hasse/pair-3.svg) |

## The diagrams

- [fano-subspaces.svg](../svg/hasse/fano-subspaces.svg): the Fano plane's subspaces, empty, the 7 points, the 7 lines, full, with 35 covering edges (7 + 21 + 7).
- [rules.svg](../svg/hasse/rules.svg): the 16 rules ordered by implication, the 4-cube, with 32 covering edges; each node carries its icon. A rule and its complement are opposite corners, four steps apart.
- `pair-0.svg` … `pair-7.svg`: each rule pair as a Hasse diagram pair, the rules on the left and the subspaces on the right, both marked.
- [lines.svg](../svg/hasse/lines.svg): the seven lines in turn (SMIL, 2 s each), each with its three points and its six rules.

## The margins

Each icon's drawing sits about 8 units in from every edge. The author: "the 8 units on each side are for non-printing control coded image filtering; if we xor those we can change the interpretation without changing the image". [margin/Fano_point_1.codes.svg](../svg/margin/Fano_point_1.codes.svg) carries the bytes 0x13 0x3f 0x1c 0x1f (DC3, ?, FS, US), one per side, as invisible one-unit cells; [margin/Fano_point_1.codes-xor-ff.svg](../svg/margin/Fano_point_1.codes-xor-ff.svg) carries them XORed with 0xff. The build checks, for all seven points, that the bytes read back, that the two margins differ by the key, and that the drawing still reads as the same point.

Measured once, 2026-10-13: rendered at ten times their size in Chrome (headless), the original Fano_point_1.svg and both copies give byte-identical pixels (SHA-256 7c1dbb9a71293b66…), while the three files differ as text. The build does not render, so it does not repeat that measurement.

E8 on its Coxeter plane, in both frames (the 240 roots, 8 rings of 30, the two parity halves of the byte): [E8.md](E8.md).
